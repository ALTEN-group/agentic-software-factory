# 🗺️ Adoption Roadmap

Adopt the model in three steps. Build experience before changing the workflow.

## Step 1: Advanced training

Train teams to work with AI. Start building Persistent Context and deterministic controls.

## Step 2: AI use cases across the whole cycle

Use AI for specific tasks without changing existing workflows or tools: meeting summaries, specifications, code, tests, documentation, and audits. Measure where it helps.

## Step 3: Transform stage by stage

Redesign one [stage](./overview) at a time around AI. Stabilize each change before moving to the next.
