# Adoption Roadmap

Adopting this operating model is a long transformation. It cannot be switched on all at once, and trying to do so is the
most reliable way to fail. It is done in three steps, and each step prepares the next.

## Step 1: Advanced training

Start with advanced training, before anything changes. People learn to work with AI at a high level. Squads start to build persistent context and deterministic controls.

## Step 2: AI use cases across the whole cycle

Then introduce AI use cases across the entire development cycle, from the client meeting to production, without changing the workflow or the tooling. People keep working the way they do today, and AI helps inside each stage. This builds experience and shows where AI helps most. Squads start to use AI in specific tasks like automated test generation, documentation updates, assisted specifications, code and audits.

## Step 3: Transform stage by stage

Only then start the transformation itself, and start slowly. Take the stages of the [operating model](./overview) one at
a time. For each stage, optimize its workflow around what AI makes possible, and move to another stage once the change
has settled.
